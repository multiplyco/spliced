The Sound tab of a film in [Build](build.md) shows what the film sounds like, part by part.

## The lines

Music, voice and effects each have their own line, laid over the music's beat grid, so you can see what lands on it. Turn the sound on to hear the parts in step with the picture.

What you hear is **Render**, the render's own sound, or **Stems**, the film's parts played one by one, so you can mute or solo any line. Soloing a line switches to Stems.

The lanes share the timeline's view: the same ruler, zoomed the same way. **Film** shows the same stretch as the timeline above, **Beat** the selected beat or the looped range. Close enough, each effect is named on its lane.

Drag across a stretch to loop it, or to leave a note about it. A note on the sound is also listed in [Build](build.md)'s Notes tab.

## Takes and stems

- A **take** is one of several versions of a sound: a voice line read again, or another try at the music. Hear one under the picture, then use it.
- A **stem** is one part of the music on its own, such as the drums or the bass.
- The **bed** is the music that runs under the film.

## Where the sound comes from

Making the sound is covered in [Voice, sound and footage](voice-and-sound.md): the voice-over line by line, the score arranged to the cuts, and the UI sounds placed on the film's cues.
