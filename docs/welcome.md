Spliced is a local app, a set of pipelines and a few skills. You add films you like, Spliced cuts them into scenes, your AI writes down what happens in each one, and you borrow the scenes you want for a film of your own.

Spliced does no AI work itself. Your own agent reads the films, writes the breakdowns, builds the film and answers your notes. Everything stays in a folder on your machine.

> Spliced works and is tested, and it is still being shaped. Expect names to move. These pages describe the tool as it is today.

## How it fits together

1. **Study.** Paste a link to a film. Spliced downloads it and runs the machine pass: cuts, tempo, palette and contact sheets, before any AI reads a frame. See [Add references](add-references.md).
2. **Name.** Your AI writes the breakdown scene by scene: what is on screen, how it moves and how to recreate it, tagged from one fixed list. See [Imports and breakdowns](imports.md).
3. **Collect.** Browse the [Library](library.md) and put the scenes you want on a [board](boards.md). A board is your film's script: one beat per scene, with its words and its length.
4. **Make.** Start a film from the board. Your AI builds it with the [film kit](film-kit.md), and you review each render in [Build](build.md), beside the scenes it borrows from.

## The shapes it comes in

| Shape | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The app | Runs in your browser, served from your own machine. `spliced setup` opens it the first time, `spliced open` after that. |
| The Mac app | The same app in a native window per project, with the engine inside it. See [The Mac app](mac-app.md). |
| The `spliced` command | Everything the app does is a command, so you, a script or your AI can run it. See [Commands](commands.md). |
| Skills and an MCP server | What your AI follows and the tools it gets. See [Connect your AI](connect-your-ai.md). |
